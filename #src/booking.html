Hut Rules

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Let us know your arrival time and we will have your hut warm and ready.

The Glass Huts are a quiet, adults-focused retreat. We keep evenings peaceful so every guest can enjoy the stillness of the forest.

Smoking is not permitted inside the huts. Open flames stay in the designated fire pit and wood-fired tub only.

Please treat the huts and their surroundings gently, so the next guest can arrive to the same untouched calm.

Cancellation Policy

Cancel free of charge up to 14 days before arrival and receive a full refund of your payment.

Cancel between 14 and 7 days before arrival and we retain 50% of the total stay.

Cancellations within 7 days of arrival are non-refundable, though dates can often be moved subject to availability.

Added services and experiences can be adjusted up to 48 hours before your stay begins.

Good to Know

Each hut sleeps two guests and includes a private bathroom, kitchenette, heating and a glass roof made for stargazing.

Parking is a short, signposted walk from the huts to keep the setting calm and car-free. We help carry your bags on arrival.

Wi-Fi is available, but we gently encourage you to disconnect and let the forest set the pace of your stay.

Have a special request or occasion in mind? Add a note at checkout and we will do our best to arrange it.
