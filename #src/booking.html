Sunrise Breakfast Basket

A locally sourced morning spread delivered to your hut door: warm sourdough, Danish cheeses, seasonal fruit, house granola and freshly brewed coffee. Enjoy it in bed or on your private terrace as the forest wakes.

Three-Course Nordic Dinner

Our chef prepares a seasonal three-course menu built around Mariager fjord seafood and produce from nearby farms, served in your hut by candlelight. Wine pairings and vegetarian options available on request.

Private Wood-Fired Hot Tub

We heat a cedar hot tub beside your hut so it is ready at dusk. Soak under an open sky, watch the stars appear over the treetops and let the day melt away. Towels and robes are included.

In-Hut Wellness Massage

A qualified therapist comes to your hut for a 60-minute deep-relaxation massage using organic Nordic oils. Choose a slot on arrival day or before dinner and unwind completely in your own space.

Guided Sunrise Forest Walk

Join a local guide for a gentle two-hour walk through the woodland around the fjord, learning to read the forest, forage in season and spot the wildlife that shares this quiet corner of Denmark.

Stargazing Kit & Star Map

Away from city light, the night sky here is extraordinary. We leave a telescope, warm blankets and a seasonal star map in your hut so you can trace constellations from your bed through the glass roof.

Hut Rules

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Let us know your arrival time and we will have your hut warm and ready.

The Glass Huts are a quiet, adults-focused retreat. We keep evenings peaceful so every guest can enjoy the stillness of the forest.

Smoking is not permitted inside the huts. Open flames stay in the designated fire pit and wood-fired tub only.

Please treat the huts and their surroundings gently, so the next guest can arrive to the same untouched calm.

Cancellation Policy

Cancel free of charge up to 14 days before arrival and receive a full refund of your payment.

Cancel between 14 and 7 days before arrival and we retain 50% of the total stay.

Cancellations within 7 days of arrival are non-refundable, though dates can often be moved subject to availability.

Added services and experiences can be adjusted up to 48 hours before your stay begins.

Good to Know

Each hut sleeps two guests and includes a private bathroom, kitchenette, heating and a glass roof made for stargazing.

Parking is a short, signposted walk from the huts to keep the setting calm and car-free. We help carry your bags on arrival.

Wi-Fi is available, but we gently encourage you to disconnect and let the forest set the pace of your stay.

Have a special request or occasion in mind? Add a note at checkout and we will do our best to arrange it.
