Frequently Asked Questions

Rates start from €320 per night and vary with the season, the hut you choose and the length of your stay. Every night includes private access to your glass cabin, the surrounding forest and the shared wellness sauna by the fjord.

Stays of three nights or more receive a reduced nightly rate, and midweek arrivals are gentler on both your wallet and your calm. Live pricing for your exact dates always appears at the final step of the booking flow.

There are no hidden fees. Cleaning, fresh linen, firewood and local taxes are already folded into the price you see, so the number you book is the number you pay.

Plans change, and we understand. Cancel at least 14 days before your arrival and you will receive a full refund, no questions asked.

Cancellations made between 14 and 7 days before check-in are refunded at fifty percent. Within 7 days of arrival the first night is non-refundable, as your hut is held exclusively for you.

If you would rather move your escape than cancel it, we are happy to reschedule once at no charge, subject to availability. Just write to us and we will find you a new date.

Each hut sits a short stroll from the parking clearing, reached by a soft forest path of roughly 300 to 500 metres. The walk is part of the arrival ritual: it lets the world fall quiet before you reach your door.

The trail is gently graded and lit at dusk by low lanterns, though sturdy shoes are wise after rain. We provide a small trolley at the car park so luggage and groceries make the journey easily.

If mobility is a concern, let us know when you book. Two of our huts sit closest to the clearing and we can arrange assistance with your bags on arrival and departure.

Every glass hut is designed for two, with one generous king bed framed by the forest and the sky. It is an intimate space, made for couples, close friends or a little solitude.

Selected huts can welcome a third guest on a comfortable sofa daybed, ideal for a small family with one child. Please note the extra guest when booking so we can prepare the room and linen for you.

For groups travelling together, several huts can be reserved side by side so you share the forest yet keep your own quiet corner. Contact us and we will arrange the neighbouring cabins.

Well-behaved dogs are warmly welcome in two of our pet-friendly huts. Simply let us know when you book so we can prepare the right cabin, a water bowl and a cosy blanket for your companion.

A small cleaning fee of €30 per stay applies, and we ask that pets are not left alone in the hut. The forest trails around the retreat are perfect for long, leisurely walks together.

To protect the calm and the wildlife, we keep the retreat dog-only and ask that pets stay leashed in shared areas. If you have any questions about travelling with your animal, just reach out before you arrive.

Check-in opens from 15:00, giving us time to air the hut and lay the fire before you arrive. Check-out is by 11:00, when the forest is at its freshest for your final morning coffee.

Arrival is contactless and calm. A few days before your stay we send a personal door code and a simple map to your hut, so you can slip straight into the quiet without a reception desk.

Arriving early or leaving late? Depending on the day we can often hold your bags or extend your hut for a small fee. Just ask, and we will do our best to make the day unhurried.
